4305 Assignment #1, Scenario #2

Main Flow:

The shift supervisor selects the date and shift they want to review.
The system displays the officers currently assigned and compares the number of scheduled officers with the required workforce level.
The system reviews the qualifications of the assigned officers and identifies any gaps in the required skill mix.
The supervisor reviews a list of available officers who meet the staffing or skill requirements.
The supervisor selects a qualified officer to add to the shift.
The system checks the selected officer's schedule for potential conflicts.
If no conflict exists, the system assigns the officer and recalculates the workforce level and skill coverage.
The supervisor reviews the updated coverage and confirms the shift.

Alternate Flow: If no qualified officers are available to fill an identified gap, the system alerts the supervisor that the shift does not meet the required coverage so additional action can be taken.
